MACRO MARCH 2025

Dew on a Dandelion — The Physics of a Perfect Macro Shot

Macro work is 90% setup and 10% trigger. For this frame, I used a macro rail on a tripod and shot 47 frames for focus stacking — the final image uses 12 of them. Wind is the enemy; I waited for 6am stillness in a Kerala garden and had exactly 20 minutes before the light shifted.

Varanasi at Blue Hour — Shooting With Permission, Not Intrusion

Cultural photography carries an ethical weight that landscape work doesn't. In Varanasi, I spent two days walking the ghats without a camera before I lifted it. This entry is about that decision — and why the restraint produced a better photograph than immediacy ever could.

PRODUCT NOVEMBER 2024

Shooting Watch Gears — Why I Never Use a Lightbox

Most product photographers reach for a lightbox first. I reach for a single snoot on a boom arm. The difference is everything: controlled specular highlights on polished metal surfaces tell a story that flat, diffused light cannot. This is how that gear shot was built — in a black room, with one light, and a lot of repositioning.
